This kit uses one family, Cormorant, for everything but code. It has more range than that sounds, because a good book face holds several voices.

## Light is for size

Cormorant was drawn as a display face, with fine hairlines that come alive at large sizes. Headings use its light weight and let scale do the work. Set a title at 60px in light and it needs no bold to be the loudest thing on the page.

## Medium is for reading

The same hairlines thin out at small sizes, so body text moves up to medium and turns on the Garamond letterforms, whose a and e have larger counters. It also sits two sizes above where a grotesque would, because the x-height is small.

## Italic is for asides

Leads, captions, quotations and the current page are italic. That gives a page a second speaker without a second font.

> A good book face holds several voices.

## Semibold is for names

A list item's name and every small label are semibold. Those are the places a reader scans, so they get the firmest strokes the family has.
