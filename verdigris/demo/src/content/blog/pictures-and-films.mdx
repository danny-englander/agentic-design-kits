A post here is one centered column, 45rem wide, like a page of a book. Markdown fills it with text. Written in MDX instead, a post can bring in a few of the kit's components and set them between its paragraphs, without leaving that column.

## A gallery

A `Gallery` shows each picture whole, at its own shape. These are rows, so the bottom edges line up across each row, and the captions sit under them in the kit's italic.

<Gallery items={gallery.slice(0, 6)} variant="rows" />

The text picks up again under it, on the same centered column.

## A film from YouTube or Vimeo

A `VideoEmbed` takes the address you'd share and a title for screen readers. The player fills the column at 16:9, and loads only as it nears the screen. Nothing plays until the reader presses play. YouTube comes from its no-cookie address and Vimeo with Do Not Track on, so neither sets a tracking cookie before then.

## Another post

A `PostCard` points to another post. It takes the post's file name and finds its title, summary, date and cover itself, so the card stays right when that post changes. Only its title is a link.
